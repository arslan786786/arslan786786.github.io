About

I study how large language models can support medication-safety decisions without trusting outdated patient information. My current work asks a simple question: is each patient fact in the record still valid at the moment a decision is made? I build retrieval-augmented systems that check this before answering, and that ask for a missing value, retrieve more evidence, or escalate to a pharmacist when answering would be unsafe.

Research

Present but Outdated: Validity-Aware Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Safer Medication Decisions from Longitudinal Patient Records Under review

Muhammad Arslan, Jungeun Kim

SafeClin-RAG checks whether every patient fact is still valid before answering a medication question, and chooses to answer, ask, retrieve or abstain. We also built MedAct-Bench, a benchmark from real NSAID orders with paired temporal traps and controls, evaluated on MIMIC-IV and hospital data.
